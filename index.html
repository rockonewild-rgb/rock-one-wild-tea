CHAPTER 01 / TERROIR & BOTANICAL SCIENCE

The Montane Cloud Microclimate of Ettampitiya

Figure 1.1: Morning cloud inversion over the Gannilawattha amphitheater slopes (1,200m+).

Granulite Mineral Bedrock

The estate is anchored atop Precambrian granulite facies metamorphic bedrock. Decomposed mica-schist creates a naturally acidic, iron-rich loam (pH 4.5–5.2) with exceptional water percolation. This deep mineral matrix forces the tea taproots deep into mountain fissures, imparting a signature crystallized honey minerality found nowhere else in South Asia.

Thermal Diurnal Shift & Mist Inversions

Daytime temperatures of 24°C plunge to 11°C at night under mountain breeze corridors originating from the central highland massifs. This intense diurnal fluctuation slows leaf growth, concentrating polyphenol and theanine syntheses in the youngest shoots while suppressing coarse bitterness.
